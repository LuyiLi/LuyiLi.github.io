Selected work

All publications
PGMT2026 · Preprint

PGMT: Perceptive General Motion Tracking for Humanoid Robots

Flat-ground trackers struggle when the terrain changes. PGMT adapts diverse whole-body motions to stairs and uneven ground, with locomotion, teleoperation, and fall recovery in one policy.
Paper Project GitHub
TAGA2026 · Preprint

TAGA: Terrain-aware Active Gaze Learning for Generalizable Agile Humanoid Locomotion

Narrow beams, gaps, and sparse footholds leave little room for error. TAGA learns to focus on the terrain that matters next, enabling agile humanoid locomotion with onboard sensing.
Paper Project YouTube
KiViIROS 2026 · Accepted

KiVi: Kinesthetic-Visuospatial Integration for Dynamic and Safe Egocentric Legged Locomotion

Glare and occlusion can undermine visual locomotion. KiVi combines vision with body sensing so a quadruped can navigate uneven terrain and stay stable when visual information degrades.
Paper Project YouTube
